Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

"Undertone uploader" is a private, single-user tool used by the owner of the Undertone YouTube channel to publish videos to that channel through the YouTube Data API.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's consent via Google sign-in, the tool uploads videos, sets thumbnails and captions, and reads basic channel statistics for the Undertone channel only.

What it stores

An OAuth token is stored locally on the owner's computer. No personal data about viewers or any third party is collected, stored, sold or shared. The tool has no users other than the channel owner.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
